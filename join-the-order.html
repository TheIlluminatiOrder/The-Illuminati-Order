THE ETERNAL OATH OF THE ILLUMINATI

To begin with, since our inception, Illuminati members have dedicated themselves to the advancement of the human race by taking an oath of commitment. Specifically, joining the Illuminati involves promises that are a fundamental tradition of the organization, established as written contracts between an individual and all members of humanity. The first pledge of the Illuminati is known as the Eternal Oath.

Furthermore, the Illuminati is often portrayed as a secret society that allegedly seeks to control world events and influence global affairs from behind the scenes. In addition, various conspiracy theories suggest that members include influential figures in politics, business, and entertainment.

Historically, it’s important to note that the term “Illuminati” refers to the Bavarian Illuminati, a group founded in 1776 in Germany with the aim of promoting Enlightenment values and combating religious and political oppression.

Today, the concept has evolved into a cultural phenomenon, characterized by numerous fictional and speculative narratives surrounding it. Consequently, many people view the idea of joining the Illuminati as a metaphor for seeking power, influence, or enlightenment, rather than a literal invitation to a secret society.
